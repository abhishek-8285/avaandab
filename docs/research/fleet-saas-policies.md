# Fleet SaaS Provider Policies & Terms — Research Summary

Research date: Aug 2026. Sources: live legal pages of Samsara (fleet IoT/telematics SaaS)
and Tookan/Jungleworks (delivery & fleet management SaaS). Intended as reference material
for drafting comparable policies/T&Cs in MVTMS. Not legal advice; verify against local law
(India — DPDP Act 2023, IT Act, motor vehicle regulations) before publishing.

## 1. Samsara — Master Terms of Service (last updated June 2026)

Source: https://www.samsara.com/legal/platform-terms-of-service

- **Acceptance**: accepting online, executing an Order Form, purchasing products, or
  merely accessing/using the products binds the customer. No-competitor clause (sole
  discretion). Representative must have authority to bind the entity.
- **Changes to Agreement**: Samsara may modify at any time, sole discretion; posting to
  services/website/email is notice; continued use = acceptance.
- **License**: non-sublicensable, non-exclusive, non-transferable, limited, revocable.
  Bounded by Order Form scope (feature scope, user count, API caps). Usage beyond licensed
  scope → charge at list price. No downgrade of license plan during term.
- **License restrictions**: no resale/white-label, no reverse engineering, no
  competitive use, no benchmarking, no storing malicious code, no illegal/defamatory/
  harassing material, no violation of 3rd-party privacy rights, no use in unsupported
  countries. Samsara may suspend/terminate immediately on suspected violation. Customer
  indemnifies Samsara for violations.
- **Product updates**: Samsara may change/discontinue features any time without notice,
  at sole discretion. If a license is discontinued before expiry without replacement,
  either party may terminate the order and request refund. Automatic updates consented.
  All feedback assigned to vendor.
- **Payment & taxes**:
  - Fees per Order Form; wire transfer default; up to 3% processing fee (waived for ACH).
  - Late payments: 1.5% per month late fee; license suspension if delinquent 30+ days.
  - All payments non-refundable unless stated. Vendor may report to credit agencies.
  - Customer pays all taxes; vendor invoices to reimburse.
- **Accounts**: customer responsible for admin/security of accounts, confidentiality of
  credentials/API tokens, notification of compromise.
- **Customer data**:
  - Customer owns data; vendor keeps confidential; vendor gets broad license to use
    (operating, supporting, improving, distributing) which survives termination.
  - Vendor maintains reasonable admin/physical/technical safeguards; no sharing without
    consent except compelled by law.
  - Data export available any time during term via dashboard/API (some data not
    exportable). On termination/non-renewal or beyond retention, data may be immediately
    deleted.
  - Customer warrants it has consents for personal data and indemnifies vendor.
  - Data Protection Addendum governs personal-data processing.
- **Confidentiality**: mutual, standard exclusions (public knowledge, prior knowledge,
  third-party right, independent development). Law disclosure allowed with advance
  notice best effort.
- **Proprietary rights**: vendor owns all IP in services; no ownership conveyed.
- **Non-Samsara (third-party) products/integrations**: customer assumes all risk; AS-IS;
  customer waives claims and indemnifies vendor.
- **Publicity**: vendor may list customer name/logo on website, press releases, customer
  lists, SEC filings, earnings calls without consent; nothing more without consent.
- **Term & renewal**:
  - Written cancellation notice 30 days before expiry, or vendor may auto-renew up to
    90 days after expiry (term up to 1 year or matching prior term).
  - Vendor may terminate for convenience any time with refund; otherwise orders locked
    to license expiration date.
- **Survival after termination**: license restrictions, feedback, payment, customer
  data, confidentiality, proprietary rights, term, warranty disclaimers, liability cap,
  dispute resolution, general terms.
- **Warranty disclaimer**: products "AS IS", no warranties; not a crash-avoidance system;
  customer solely responsible for speeding/tolls/traffic violations; supervision
  required even when products in use.
- **Limitation of liability**:
  - No consequential/indirect damages (lost profits, data loss, service interruption).
  - Aggregate cap = fees paid in preceding 12 months, or $100 for free-trial users.
  - Excluded from cap: customer indemnification, license restriction breach, payment
    obligations.
- **Disputes**: 60-day resolution period; Samsara elects venue — JAMS confidential
  arbitration in San Francisco (English) or SF state/federal courts. Class-action waiver.
  California law. Fee-shifting favoring vendor (prevailing party, settlement-offer rule,
  one-day fee arbitration in Nevada).
- **Acceptable use**: no harmful/discriminatory/illegal use, no human-rights violations,
  no abusive/harassing conduct toward vendor staff. Abuse report channel.
- **Export restrictions**: US export/sanctions compliance; SDN lists.
- **Force majeure**: standard (war, act of God, epidemics, government action, utility/
  comms failure).
- **Assignment**: customer cannot assign without consent; vendor may freely.
- **No third-party beneficiaries**; vendor affiliates/employees shielded from claims.

## 2. Samsara — Hosted Software SLA (last updated Aug 2024)

Source: https://www.samsara.com/legal/hosted-software-sla

- **Commitment**: 99.99% monthly uptime for hosted software dashboard.
- **Downtime definition**: customer unable to log into dashboard due to hosted-software
  failure (hardware device failures excluded — covered by hardware warranty).
- **Service credits** (per calendar month):

  | Uptime | Credit |
  |---|---|
  | <99.99% to ≥99.9% | 3 days |
  | <99.9% to ≥99.0% | 7 days |
  | <99.0% to ≥90.0% | 15 days |
  | <90.0% | 30 days |

- **Claim**: written notice within 30 days of eligibility, else forfeit. Max credit
  30 days/month.
- **Exclusions**: force majeure, customer/third-party infrastructure, customer breach,
  cellular/internet providers, hardware issues, anything not solely hosted-software
  failure.
- **Exclusive remedy**: SLA credits are the sole remedy for uptime failure.

## 3. Tookan (Jungleworks) — Terms of Service

Source: https://jungleworks.com/tookan/terms-of-service/

- **Binding**: using the service = agreement; binds employer entity too (representative
  warrants age of majority, authority).
- **Service changes**: may terminate/suspend/modify services without cause or prior
  notice; may delete any content/data at discretion. Site content copyright-protected.
- **Overdue accounts**: if amounts overdue 30+ days (10+ days for credit-card charges),
  vendor may accelerate all unpaid obligations and suspend service; minimum 6 days'
  prior notice of overdue status.
- **AS-IS clause**: products obtained without a signed agreement provided "AS-IS" with no
  warranties.
- **Data security / user data**: services collect GPS and sensor data (coordinates,
  phone numbers, names, addresses) from field workers and customers. Vendor shares data:
  with service-providing third parties, to comply with law/legal process (endeavours to
  notify), to protect vendor rights, in emergencies, for business deals (M&A).
  Customer can access and delete its user data. Customer must give its own users notices
  and obtain consents (privacy policy / cookies). Customer's user data is NOT covered by
  Tookan's privacy policy — customer must provide its own.
- **Governing law**: California (US parties: SF courts). Non-US parties: confidential
  binding arbitration in San Francisco under AAA International Arbitration Rules.
  IP-violation exceptions allow injunctive relief in California courts.
- **Limitation of liability**: no direct, indirect, incidental, exemplary, special or
  consequential damages (lost profits, business interruption, data loss), even if
  advised.
- **Third-party links**: not endorsed, not vendor's responsibility.
- **Assignment**: customer may not assign/sublicense without written consent (attempt
  void); vendor may freely transfer/assign.
- **Modification**: vendor may modify terms/services any time with or without notice by
  posting; continued use = acceptance.
- **Severability / entire agreement**: standard.

## 4. Jungleworks — Privacy Policy (applies to Tookan, Yelo, etc.)

Source: https://jungleworks.com/privacy-policy

- **Scope**: jungleworks.com and all product sites; owned by Click Labs Inc (Tampa, FL).
- **Changes**: may change any time, effective on posting; notice displayed on site and
  by email; usage consistent with policy in effect when data was submitted unless
  consented otherwise.
- **Data collection**: registration/login info, browsing data; call logs recorded in CRM
  for calls through mobile network.
- **Purposes**: usability, personalised content, offers/updates, satisfaction surveys.
- **Sharing**: with third parties working on vendor's behalf; to comply with law (vendor
  endeavours to notify); to protect vendor rights/property; emergencies (personal
  safety); business deals (merger, financing, acquisition, divestiture, bankruptcy).
- **Data retention**: platform report/data accessible for 12 months from current date of
  viewing; older data charged additionally on mutual discussion.
- **Rights (PII)**: access, rectification, erasure (subject to legal retention),
  restriction of processing, data portability, objection, and no-sale of personal info.
- **Security**: commercially reasonable measures; no absolute guarantee.
- **Consent**: use of website = consent to policy.

## 5. Fleetx (India) — Terms of Service & DPDPA Privacy Policy

Source: https://www.fleetx.ai/terms-of-service , https://www.fleetx.ai/privacy-policy
Fleetx Technologies Private Limited, Gurugram (Haryana). The most India-relevant
reference — DPDP Act 2023 compliant. SLA 99.95% uptime; ISO 27001:2013, SOC 2 certified.

### Terms of Service highlights (India-specific)

- **Acceptance**: filling Order Form or availing services = acceptance of Terms as
  amended; accessing website binds to terms + applicable laws.
- **Material/transaction terms**: device swap rules (2 vehicles = 1 swap); SIM used for
  non-GPS purposes charged as per usage; reinstallation/removal turnaround 48–72 hrs at
  key locations; repair within 72 hrs of ticket (client must report fault, else no
  liability); data accessible up to 1 year on platform, +1 year on request.
- **Change requests**: in writing within 48–72 hrs of PO; vendor response = acceptance,
  silence = deemed rejected.
- **Pricing/payment**: per Order Form; pre-approved travel/courier expenses reimbursed;
  prior written authorization needed for expenses above INR 5000.
- **Term & termination**: **24-month lock-in** from execution; auto-renewal after
  lock-in; termination by either party with 60 days' written notice post lock-in;
  survival clauses (confidentiality, limitation of liability, non-solicitation,
  mediation/arbitration, governing law).
- **Warranties**: device warranty 12 months from installation or manufacturer warranty,
  whichever earlier; excludes water/dust/physical damage caused by client personnel;
  devices must be returned on discontinuation or charged at new-device cost.
- **Limitation of liability**: no special/indirect/incidental/consequential damages
  (except breach of confidentiality); **aggregate cap INR 10,000/-**.
- **Independent contractor** clause; no employee-employer relationship (driver/agent
  status protection).
- **Non-solicitation**: client and affiliates may not solicit vendor employees or
  customers during term + 12 months after.
- **Dispute resolution**: mediation within 1 month, then sole arbitrator under the
  **Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996**; venue/seat **Gurgaon, Haryana, India**;
  language English; costs shared equally.
- **Governing law**: laws of Haryana, India.
- **Force majeure**: fire, riot, strike, terror, war, epidemic, pandemic, lockdowns,
  movement restrictions, changes in government policy/regulations.
- **NDA section (Section III)**: confidentiality 3 years from disclosure; criminal
  liability for breach of trust under **IPC Section 405** and breach of confidentiality
  under **IT Act 2000, Section 72**; return of materials within 7 working days.
- **App permissions (India context)**: Device/App History, Location, Background
  Location (consent-based, used for duty-wise KM, tracking, EPOD), Phone, Contacts,
  Camera/Photo, Device ID & Call info. (Privacy policy v2.0 also covers Apple TrueDepth
  liveness verification for attendance — no biometric storage.)

### Privacy Policy highlights (DPDPA-compliant, effective 2026-07-30, V2.0)

- **Governing law**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 + rules + IT Act 2000.
  English + Hindi versions per DPDPA Section 5(3) (Eighth Schedule).
- **Roles**: Fleetx = Data Fiduciary; users = Data Principals; affiliate sharing treated
  as disclosure under Section 6 of policy.
- **Legal basis (DPDPA s.7)**: consent, or legitimate uses — voluntary data for specified
  purpose (with opt-out), compliance with Indian law/judgments, medical emergencies,
  epidemics/disasters/public order, employment purposes.
- **Consent**: free, specific, informed, unconditional, unambiguous; withdrawal as easy
  as giving (cookie centre or email); withdrawal doesn't affect prior processing;
  processing ceases + erasure within reasonable time unless legally required to retain.
- **Disclosure categories**: cloud/hosting, payment processors, communication/support
  platforms, analytics, professional advisers/auditors, government/regulatory
  authorities. No sharing with non-related third parties without consent. Processors
  bound contractually to documented instructions + equivalent safeguards.
- **International transfers**: only per DPDPA s.16 and Central Government restrictions;
  sectoral rules (payment data) prevail; vendor remains accountable for offshore
  processors. Primary storage in India; AI features may use authorised AI providers
  under contract.
- **Security/retention**: encryption/obfuscation/tokenisation; access control; logging
  with 180-day retention default; backups; breach-resilience. Retention only as long as
  needed for purpose/compliance/disputes, then erased or anonymised.
- **Breach notification (DPDPA s.8(6))**: notify Data Protection Board of India without
  delay; furnish particulars within 72 hours; notify affected Data Principals without
  delay in clear language (nature, extent, timing, consequences, mitigation, self-help
  measures, contact).
- **Children**: no tracking/behavioural monitoring/targeted ads to children (<18);
  verifiable parental/guardian consent required; deletion if processed without consent.
- **Data Principal rights (DPDPA s.11-15)**: information about processing, correction/
  completion/updating/erasure, consent withdrawal, **nomination of representative**
  (on death/incapacity), summary of processed data, grievance redressal, complaint to
  Data Protection Board of India. Grievance acknowledged within 3 business days,
  responded within 30 days. Opt-out within 7 business days. Duties of principal under
  s.15 (no impersonation, no false info, no frivolous complaints).
- **Grievance Officer (DPDPA s.5(8))**: name + designation + address + email published
  (Sanjeev Kumar Mehta, VP Finance & Legal, Gurugram; grievance@fleetx.ai). DPO
  contact also published (dpo@fleetx.ai).
- **Refund policy**: refund only when Service not provided; valid requests within
  stipulated time window per service/policy; beyond window = ineligible.

## 6. Checklist — policies to replicate in MVTMS (incl. India-specific)

Suggested adaptations for this project (adjust to Indian law and local practice):

1. **Customer-facing T&C (terms of service)** — acceptance model, service scope,
   prohibited uses, liability cap (e.g. 12-month fees), no-consequential-damages,
   governing law/jurisdiction (Indian courts), force majeure, severability, assignment,
   amendment-with-notice, survival clauses.
2. **Privacy policy** — data collected (driver/vehicle/customer PII), purposes, sharing
   with vendors (Razorpay etc.), legal disclosure, retention window (e.g. 12 months
   free, paid beyond), PII rights under DPDP Act 2023 (access/correction/erasure), data
   export/delete capability, no data sale.
3. **SLA** — 99.9% monthly uptime target (or lower for on-prem), service credits
   tiering, 30-day claim window, exclusions (maintenance, force majeure, network),
   exclusive-remedy clause.
4. **Subscription/billing policy** — auto-renewal with 30-day cancellation notice,
   non-refundable prepaid fees, case-by-case refunds with processing-fee deduction,
   1.5%/month late fee, 30-day delinquency suspension, overdue acceleration, credit
   reporting.
5. **Data deletion on termination** — export before expiry; deletion after retention
   period; document retention windows.
6. **Acceptable use policy** — no illegal/abusive use, no resale/white-label, no
   reverse engineering of platform, misuse reporting channel.
7. **API/third-party integration terms** — AS-IS integrations, customer indemnity.
8. **Customer of customer (C2C) privacy** — like Tookan: platform provides tools;
   customer must give its end-users notices and obtain consents; platform not
   responsible for customer's user data privacy compliance.
9. **Publicity clause** — permission to list customer name/logo with opt-out.

### India-specific additions (from Fleetx)

10. **DPDP Act 2023 privacy policy** — designate Data Fiduciary; publish Grievance
    Officer (name, designation, address, email) + DPO contact; English + Hindi versions
    (s.5(3)); consent with clear affirmative action; withdrawal-as-easy-as-giving;
    legitimate-use bases (s.7); breach notification to Data Protection Board within
    72 hrs + affected-user notice; Data Principal rights incl. nomination (s.11-15);
    acknowledgement 3 business days / response 30 days; children <18 verifiable consent;
    international transfer rules (s.16); retention limits + erasure/anonymisation.
11. **Arbitration clause** — sole arbitrator under Arbitration and Conciliation Act,
    1996; seat in your HQ jurisdiction (e.g., Gurugram/Haryana pattern); English;
    mediation-first optional.
12. **Confidentiality/NDA** — 3-year term, return of materials in 7 working days,
    criminal liability reference (IPC s.405 breach of trust, IT Act s.72) — check
    current applicability before adopting.
13. **Liability cap in INR** — Fleetx caps at INR 10,000 absolute; consider a higher
    figure (e.g., 12-month fees) unless contract value is small; consequential-damages
    exclusion with confidentiality carve-out.
14. **Lock-in + auto-renewal** — 24-month lock-in pattern common in Indian fleet
    hardware/SaaS; 60-day written termination notice after lock-in.
15. **Force majeure including India-specific events** — epidemics, pandemics,
    lockdowns, movement restrictions, changes in government policy/regulations.
16. **Refund eligibility policy** — refund only if service not provided; submission
    window per service; late claims ineligible.
17. **App permission disclosures** — enumerate Android/iOS permissions (location,
    background location, camera, contacts, device ID) + purpose, consent-based, per
    device-platform guidance.
18. **Data retention** — platform data accessible 1 year + 1 year on request (Fleetx
    pattern); align with MVTMS invoice/trip record needs and IT Act records rules.
